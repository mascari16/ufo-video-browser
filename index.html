PURSUE Videos
Add videos from a newer release

When war.gov posts a new release, paste its videos here as a JSON array and they join the grid in this browser. Fields: id, release, releaseDate (YYYY-MM-DD), location, captured (YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD), duration (seconds), agency, sensor, title, description, plus dvids, dod, ym from the DVIDS thumbnail address if you want playback.

Data compiled from the PURSUE releases on war.gov/UFO (Releases 01–08, May 8 – Nov 20, 2026; Release 07 had no videos). Capture dates are as precise as the official listings give. Thumbnails and playback stream directly from DVIDS, the Defense Department's video service that war.gov uses; nothing is downloaded. If a thumbnail can't load, a drawn sensor-style stand-in is shown. New videos are checked for daily and added automatically. Official database: war.gov/UFO/?type=.vid
